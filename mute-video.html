The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop a video

    The audio stream is detected and shown.

  2. Mute it

    The video data isn't re-encoded — the picture stays exactly as it was.

  3. Download

    A silent version, usually produced within seconds.

Questions people actually ask

Is the video quality affected?

No. This removes an entire stream; the picture stream is copied untouched, byte for byte.

Can I replace the audio instead?

Not here — muting is the safe, common operation. Mixing new audio in deserves a proper editor.
