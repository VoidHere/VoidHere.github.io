The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop your audio files

    Batch is fine — an album of WAVs to MP3 takes a few minutes, unattended.

  2. Choose format and bitrate

    MP3 at 192 for general use; WAV or FLAC when something downstream needs lossless.

  3. Convert and download

    Files come out one by one, or all together in a ZIP.

Questions people actually ask

Can conversion add quality back?

No — a 128 kbps MP3 re-saved at 320 is still 128 kbps of real information in a bigger coat. Lossless outputs from lossless inputs are the only honest upgrade.

What about M4A and AAC?

Both work. M4A is AAC in an Apple-shaped box — common from phones and voice memos.
