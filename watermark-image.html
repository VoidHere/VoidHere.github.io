Questions people actually ask

Can I use a transparent PNG logo?

Yes — transparency is respected. Scale it down as a percentage of the photo so it fits consistently.

Can the watermark be tiled across the whole image?

Single placement for now, corner or centre. Tiling gets busy fast and adds options nobody uses twice.
