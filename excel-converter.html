The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop a spreadsheet or tabular file

    XLSX, XLS, ODS in — or CSV/JSON to build a workbook.

  2. Choose the direction and sheet

    Multi-sheet workbooks let you convert one sheet at a time, or all of them at once.

  3. Download

    CSV or JSON for outgoing data; a proper .xlsx for anything headed to Excel.

Questions people actually ask

Are formulas evaluated?

Cached values are used — what you last saw in Excel is what converts. The formulas themselves live on in the original file.

My dates changed format!

The classic Excel-dates-in-CSV problem. Dates export as readable ISO values; if a column looks wrong, check the sheet's own formatting first.
