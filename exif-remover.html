The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop your photos

    Batch works — holiday albums welcome.

  2. Optionally, check first

    The EXIF viewer shows exactly what's about to be removed, if you're curious.

  3. Strip and download

    Fresh files with no EXIF. They look exactly the same — only the hidden metadata goes away.

Questions people actually ask

Does stripping change how the photo looks?

No. Orientation is baked in correctly before removal, so the photo won't suddenly rotate weirdly in other apps.

Should I remove metadata from every photo?

Any photo headed for a public place — marketplaces, forums, social media after they've re-compressed it — is safer stripped. Your own archive copy can keep everything.
