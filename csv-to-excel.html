Convert CSV to Excel (XLSX)

CSV files straight into real spreadsheets — proper types, sensible column widths, multiple CSVs as multiple sheets.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop one or more CSVs

    Several files become several sheets in one workbook.

  2. Confirm the parsing

    The preview shows how columns and numbers were read.

  3. Download the workbook

    Opens cleanly in Excel, Sheets, LibreOffice — you know the drill.

Questions people actually ask

Do the column widths get set?

Auto widths are applied so you don't open a workbook of ##### instantly. Fine-tuning remains a human pleasure.

Semicolon-separated CSVs?

The separator is detected automatically (comma, semicolon or tab), with a manual override if the detection guesses wrong.
