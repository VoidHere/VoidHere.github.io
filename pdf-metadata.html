Edit PDF metadata

Fix the title, author and keywords that applications show for a PDF — or clear leftovers before sharing the file.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop the PDF

    The current title, author, subject and keywords are read out and shown in the fields.

  2. Edit or clear

    Change anything you like. Empty fields are removed from the file, not written as blanks.

  3. Save the copy

    Only metadata changes; the pages are untouched.

Questions people actually ask

What gets shown to readers?

The title appears in the browser tab and some readers; the author and keywords show up in file properties and search indexes.

Can this hide that a PDF was edited?

No, and it isn't meant to. It fixes metadata — dates and producer records are left alone.
