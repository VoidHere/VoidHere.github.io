Resize images

Exact pixels or a percentage — set the width, height, or both, and keep the aspect ratio if you want it kept.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop your images

    The dimensions appear next to each file.

  2. Set new dimensions

    Type a width, a height, or a percentage. With 'keep ratio' on, giving one side fills the other automatically.

  3. Resize and download

    Everything resizes consistently, ready to download.

Questions people actually ask

How is the quality after an upscale?

Scaling up can't invent detail that wasn't there. It won't blur like a phone screenshot — Lanczos-quality resampling is used — but 2x is the practical limit.

Can I resize by exact percentage?

Yes — switch to percent mode and set 50% to halve every dimension, handy for @2x assets.
