Compress a video

Big video, smaller file, still watchable. Dial in the quality and see the savings before you commit.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop a video

    The current size is your baseline.

  2. Pick a quality level

    High keeps things visually identical. Medium is the sweet spot — often half the size. Low is for 'send it over any connection'.

  3. Optionally shrink the frame

    720p is plenty for messaging and most viewing. Then compress and compare the numbers.

Questions people actually ask

Why is my compression slow?

Video compression is genuinely heavy work, and this runs on your CPU rather than a rack of servers. A one-minute clip at Medium takes a couple of minutes.

Will the audio survive?

Yes, with a modest audio bitrate so it doesn't undo the savings.
