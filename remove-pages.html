Delete pages from a PDF

Drop the pages you don't want — blank scans, cover sheets, that one page with the wrong total.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop the PDF

    The total page count appears so you can double-check against the document.

  2. Choose pages to remove

    Type numbers or ranges, like 1,4,7-9. Everything else stays untouched.

  3. Download the cleaned file

    You get a new PDF; the original on your disk stays as it was.

Questions people actually ask

Can I delete the first page only?

Type 1 and press the button. That's it.

What if I remove every page?

The tool refuses — a PDF needs at least one page — and tells you so instead of producing a broken file.
