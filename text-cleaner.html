Clean up text

The tidy-up pass: remove duplicate lines, sort, trim whitespace, kill empty lines, fix curly quotes.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Paste the messy text

    Exports, scraped lists, spreadsheet columns pasted as walls.

  2. Tick the fixes you want

    They stack — remove duplicates, sort alphabetically or numerically, trim each line, drop empties, straighten smart quotes.

  3. Copy the clean version

    Line count before and after, so you can see the damage undone.

Questions people actually ask

What does 'sort naturally' mean?

Numbers sort as numbers, so 'file2' comes before 'file10' — unlike the dictionary order that infuriates everyone.

Duplicate removal — case-sensitive?

Your call, via a toggle. 'Apple' and 'apple' can count as one line or two.
