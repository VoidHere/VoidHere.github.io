How it works

  1. Drop your images

    Single or batch.

  2. Choose the angle

    Quick buttons for the two right turns and the half turn, or type any angle — 3° of straightening on a crooked photo, say.

  3. Download

    Batch results arrive zipped or individually.

Questions people actually ask

Does a custom angle crop the corners?

No — the canvas grows to fit the rotated image, so nothing is cut off. The new file's dimensions adjust accordingly.

Does rotating lose quality?

Right-angle turns are mathematically lossless. Free angles resample once, which at normal quality settings is invisible.
