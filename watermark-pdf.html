Add a watermark to a PDF

Stamp text across the pages — DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, your company name — at the angle and opacity you set.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop the PDF

    The page count and size are read automatically.

  2. Write the watermark

    Type your text, then set size, colour, opacity and angle. Diagonal and faint is the classic look; your call.

  3. Apply and download

    The watermark is drawn on every page you choose.

Questions people actually ask

Can I watermark only some pages?

Yes — type the pages to cover, like 1-3,7. Leave it blank for every page.

Can I use a logo image as a watermark?

Text only for now. Image watermarks need alpha handling that would bloat this page without adding much.
