Convert Unix timestamps

Seconds or milliseconds in, human date out — and the reverse — plus the current time for reference.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Paste a number

    10 digits is seconds, 13 is milliseconds. The tool detects which and says so.

  2. Or paste a date

    ISO strings and common date formats convert in the other direction.

  3. Copy what you need

    Local time, UTC, ISO 8601 — all shown at once, so you can grab the format your system wants.

Questions people actually ask

Why does my timestamp look two decades off?

The seconds-versus-milliseconds mix-up — the single most common bug in timestamp history. The tool labels which one it read, so you'll see the mistake immediately.

What's the 2038 problem?

The date when 32-bit systems run out of seconds. Watch it happen here, live, if you paste 2147483647.
