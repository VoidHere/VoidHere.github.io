The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop an image or scanned PDF

    PNG, JPG or a PDF — scanned pages are rendered and read page by page.

  2. Let it read

    First use downloads the recognition engine (~11 MB, once). Then it works through the pages, with progress shown.

  3. Take the text

    Copy it, download as .txt, and fix the inevitable couple of misreads — 'rn' versus 'm' remains a human specialty.

Questions people actually ask

Which languages?

English for now. More languages mean more megabytes, so they're added one at a time rather than all at once.

How accurate is it?

Clean digital renders and sharp scans: excellent, better than 99% on normal text. Phone photos of curved paper: entirely readable, with a few corrections needed.

Is the text going anywhere?

No. Recognition runs in the tab, on your machine — which is also why it takes real seconds instead of a server's instant score. The trade is understood and accepted here.
