Convert TIFF to PNG or JPG

Scanners love TIFF. Browsers don't. Bridge the gap without installing anything.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop your TIFF files

    Single or multi-page; each page becomes its own image.

  2. Choose the format

    PNG keeps every pixel; JPG keeps your disk happy.

  3. Download the pages

    Multi-page archives arrive zipped.

Questions people actually ask

What kind of TIFFs are supported?

The common ones from scanners and photo tools. Exotic colour spaces may come out oddly — if so, tell the tool (contact page) and it can be looked at.

Can I keep it as TIFF?

The point is to escape TIFF, but if you need TIFF output, any image editor will re-save it from the PNG.
