Extract audio from video

Pull the soundtrack out of a video — MP3 for everything, WAV when it needs to be raw.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop a video

    Anything the engine can read — MP4, WebM, MOV, and friends.

  2. Choose format and quality

    192 kbps MP3 is transparent for most ears. 320 for music. WAV for editing or archiving.

  3. Download the audio

    The video file itself is untouched; you just gain the soundtrack.

Questions people actually ask

Can I extract from a protected or rented video?

No, and the tool won't pretend otherwise — copy protection exists precisely to stop this step, and the engine will simply fail to read it.

Which quality should I pick?

Voice: 128 is plenty. Music: 192–320. Archiving or editing: WAV, no question.
