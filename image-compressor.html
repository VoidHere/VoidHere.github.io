Questions people actually ask

How much smaller will my files get?

Photos typically drop 50–80% at quality 80. Flat graphics and screenshots compress less — they were already efficient — so try WebP output for those.

Does compressing lose quality?

At 80+, essentially never perceptibly. Below 60 you're trading visible quality for size — the preview will show you before you commit.
