Questions people actually ask

What DPI should I use?

150 DPI is fine for viewing and email. 300 DPI is the print standard. 72 DPI only if you truly need thumbnails.

Why is my output blurry?

It isn't — check your DPI setting. At 72 DPI a full A4 page is only 595 pixels wide; that's the setting, not the conversion.
