Extract a ZIP archive

Open a ZIP and take what you need — the whole thing, or only the files you actually wanted.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop a ZIP file

    The contents list appears with folder structure and sizes.

  2. Browse inside

    Pull out one file, or a selection — no need to unpack a 200-file archive to reach one PDF.

  3. Download

    Individual files directly, or everything as a fresh ZIP if you want the structure kept.

Questions people actually ask

Other archive formats?

ZIP is what this reads — the rest of the world's RAR/7z files will need a desktop tool; they're mostly compressed data with more exotic algorithms.

What about password-protected ZIPs?

Not supported — and honestly, encryption worth having is worth the desktop tool that handles it properly.
