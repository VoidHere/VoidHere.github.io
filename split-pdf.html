Split a PDF into separate files

Cut one PDF into several. Split at every page, or pull out just the ranges you name.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop the PDF

    One file at a time. The page count is shown as soon as it loads.

  2. Pick how to split

    Every page becomes its own file, or type ranges like 1-3, 4, 7-9 to choose exactly what gets cut out.

  3. Download

    You'll get each piece as its own PDF, plus a ZIP button if you'd rather take them all at once.

Questions people actually ask

How do I split at a certain page?

Use ranges: '1-5, 6-10' gives you two files, one for each range. '1,2,3' gives you three single-page files.

Does splitting change the pages?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are — text stays text, images stay sharp.
