Using the site

DigitPDF is free to use for personal and commercial work. No attribution is required for the files you create with it, and the tools place no watermark on your output. You can use them for client work, school work, business documents — anything lawful.

Your files are yours

Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you process through the site is claimed, seen, stored or transmitted by it. See the privacy policy for the full (short) explanation.

Back up anything you care about

The tools work on copies held in the page — they save a new file and never modify your original in place. Even so, every file operation in the world carries some risk of an unhappy outcome: a wrong setting, a rare bug, a browser crash at the wrong moment. For important or irreplaceable files, keep a second copy before and after you work on them. This is standard care, stated plainly because it matters.
