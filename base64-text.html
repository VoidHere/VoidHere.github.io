How it works

  3. Copy the result

    Also available in URL-safe Base64 if you're feeding it into a query string.

Questions people actually ask

Why is Base64 longer than the original?

It needs about 33% more characters — that's the cost of representing any bytes with 64 safe symbols. It's encoding, not compression.

Is Base64 encryption?

Emphatically no. It hides nothing from anyone who cares to look. Passwords and secrets belong in a password manager, not in Base64.
