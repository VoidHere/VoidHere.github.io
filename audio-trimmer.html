Trim audio

Cut a ringtone out of a song, a clip out of a lecture, a mistake out of a recording.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop an audio file

    The duration and format are shown immediately.

  2. Set the cut

    Start and end in seconds or m:ss. Play your original alongside to find the spot.

  3. Trim and download

    You get the clip in the same format as the original.

Questions people actually ask

Can I fade in or out?

Not built in — trimming is a clean cut by design. A DAW or Audacity will do fades better than a checkbox here ever would.

Does trimming an MP3 re-compress it?

Only the cut edges are re-encoded, the frames between are copied. In practice: inaudible.
