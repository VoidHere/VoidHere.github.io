This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

  3. Convert and download

Questions people actually ask

Are the conversions done online?

No — your browser has a full image engine built in, and that's what's used. The files never touch a server because there isn't one.

Why did my PNG turn white around the edges?

JPG has no transparency. If a transparent PNG goes to JPG, the background has to become something — white is the default. Output to PNG or WebP to keep transparency.
