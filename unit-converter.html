Questions people actually ask

Why is Fahrenheit↔Celsius different?

Temperature has an offset, not just a multiplier — 0° doesn't mean 'no temperature'. It's handled correctly, unlike the multiplying-by-1.8 school of conversion.

Data units — 1024 or 1000?

Both shown where it matters: decimal (1000, what drive makers print) and binary (1024, what the file system means). The eternal discrepancy, finally on speaking terms.
