Convert video formats

MP4, WebM and MOV — the three the web actually speaks. Swap between them without a desktop app.

Runs on your device No uploads, ever Free, no account
The fine print

This page has no server behind it. Files are read, changed and re-built by your own browser — then they disappear when you close the tab. That's the whole privacy policy, applied.

How it works

  1. Drop a video

    Almost any common format is readable.

  2. Pick the target

    MP4 (H.264) is the universal choice. WebM is web-native and smaller for screen recordings. MOV for Apple editing chains.

  3. Convert and download

    Quality presets keep it simple: High, Balanced, Small.

Questions people actually ask

Why can't I convert to AVI or WMV?

Those formats are comfortably past their usefulness for the web, and the engine here is kept lean on purpose. MP4 has been the sane answer since 2008.

What's the best format for a website?

MP4/H.264 for compatibility everywhere, WebM as a smaller second source if you want to serve the best to modern browsers.
