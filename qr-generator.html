The fine print

How it works

  1. Type or paste the content

    A URL, a phone number, Wi-Fi credentials in their standard format, an SMS template, a chunk of text.

  2. Style it

    Foreground and background colours, output size, and the quiet-zone margin that scanners quietly need.

  3. Download PNG or SVG

    SVG for printing at any size without a single pixel of blur.

Questions people actually ask

Low contrast colours — bad idea?

Dark-on-light with real contrast scans reliably; light-on-dark and low-contrast pairs fail on many phone cameras. The tool warns if you drift into risky territory.

How much text fits?

Comfortably: a few hundred characters. At that point the code gets dense and needs to be printed bigger to scan from a distance.
